# Sources

The catalog was assembled from public developer and designer discussion of AI-generated web design, collected in November 2026. It reflects community observation, not a controlled study. Where numbers are quoted elsewhere they come from the cited authors, not from this repository.

- Y Combinator, "Design Experts Review Vibe Coded Websites" (video), as covered by Search Engine Journal, "7 AI Vibe Coding Website Mistakes To Avoid": purple gradients, scroll fades, gratuitous hover effects, generic dashboards.
- Alan West, DEV Community: "Why Every AI-Built Website Looks the Same: Blame Tailwind's Indigo-500" and "How to Fix the 'AI-Generated' Look in Your Frontend": origin of the indigo default, feedback loop through training data, lint-style bans.
- Ken Imoto, DEV Community, "Why AI-Generated UIs Are All the Same Color": 480-experiment write-up on blue-purple bias.
- Paul Nguyen, DEV Community, "Why Every AI-Generated Template Looks the Same": Inter or Roboto, purple-blue gradient, rounded-xl cards, fixed section order.
- Adrian Krebs, "Scoring 500 Show HN pages for AI design patterns" (also summarized by Gigazine): measurement of 15 patterns, with the caveat that patterns do not prove AI authorship.
- JCarterJohnson/vibecoded-design-tells (GitHub): Reddit-mined ranking of what people name as vibe-coded tells, including default shadcn/Tailwind, AI purple, emoji icons, and the replacement "tasteful default" trap.
- Laith0003/ux-skill wiki, "How to detect AI slop in your design" and "Anti AI slop ban list": size-ladder fingerprint, forbidden gradient values.
- Alexander Mia, DEV Community, "I Got Sick of My Claude Code UI Slop": the recurring five Lucide icons, CSS giveaways, scoring approach.
- funboy322/avoid-ai-design, Nutlope/hallmark, pbakaus/impeccable (GitHub): existing anti-slop skills and catalogs; this repository differs by bundling a dependency-free scanner and a build-first brief workflow.
- Anthropic, "Improving frontend design through Skills": the distributional convergence framing and the idea of steering through reusable guidance.
- The Fountain Institute, Towards AI, Kosta Canatselis (hey.com), 925Studios, MindStudio, superdesign.dev: emoji icons, centering, glow effects, font-first fix.
